# Redstart trademark policy

Redstart's source code is licensed under the GNU Affero General Public License,
version 3 ([LICENSE](LICENSE)). That license covers the code. It does not
grant any right to the project's name or its mascot, which are covered by this
policy instead. AGPL-3.0 section 7(e) allows exactly this separation. Firefox,
Thunderbird and Rust work the same way: anyone can fork the code, but a fork has
to ship under its own name.

## What this policy covers

- **The names** "Redstart", "Redstart Nest" and "Redstart Twig", and any name
  confusingly similar to them when used for software.
- **The mascot artwork**: the drawings in
  [`redstart-nest/assets/mascot/`](redstart-nest/assets/mascot/), and every
  icon, favicon, splash screen and image generated from them (by
  `scripts/make-icon.mjs`, `scripts/make-mascot-assets.mjs`, or anything
  else).

These are trademarks of Patrick Carswell. They are not currently registered.

The mascot artwork is **not** licensed under AGPL-3.0. Copyright in it is
reserved. You may use it only as this policy allows.

## What you may do without asking

- **Talk about Redstart.** Write about it, review it, teach it, link to it, or
  compare other software to it.
- **Build and run it unmodified**, with its name and icons, for yourself or
  your organisation.
- **Redistribute unmodified official builds**, or builds of an unmodified
  official release tag, under the Redstart name.
- **Change it for your own use**, including inside your organisation, without
  renaming it, as long as you don't distribute it or offer it to people outside
  your organisation over a network.
- **Describe a fork accurately**, for example "Foo is based on Redstart" or
  "a fork of Redstart", in plain text, in a way that doesn't suggest the fork is
  Redstart or is endorsed by it.

## What needs permission

- Distributing a **modified** version, or offering one to other people over a
  network, under the Redstart name or with the mascot artwork. A fork has to
  choose its own name and replace the mascot drawings before it ships. The
  icon scripts build from those drawings, so replacing two text files replaces
  every generated icon.
- Using the name or mascot in the name, logo or branding of another product,
  service, company or domain.
- Anything that suggests your product, service or fork is official, endorsed,
  certified or supported by Redstart when it isn't.
- Selling merchandise with the name or mascot.

To ask, open an issue at
<https://github.com/ImDeadWeight/redstart-project/issues>.

## Why

If someone ships a changed version under the same name, users can't tell
whether a bug, a security hole or a missing feature is Redstart's or the
fork's. Keeping the name for the real thing protects users. It also protects
the project's reputation.

This policy isn't legal advice, and it may be revised. The version in the
repository's default branch is the one in effect.
