## How to sign

Put this line in the description of Your first pull request, with Your name and
GitHub username filled in:

> I have read the Redstart Contributor License Agreement, version 1.0, and I
> agree to it for this and all my future contributions to Redstart.
> — *Full name*, *@github-username*

If You contribute on behalf of an employer or another organisation, also name
the organisation and confirm You are authorised to sign for it.

A pull request without this line can't be merged. One signature covers all Your
later contributions to Redstart under this version of the agreement.
