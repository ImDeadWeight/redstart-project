# Software Licence and Support Agreement — Kestrel Analytics

Vendor reference: KX-4471-Q

## Scope
Kestrel licenses its forecasting platform for up to 40 named users and
provides second-line support during business hours.

## Term
Thirty-six months, with an annual price review capped at CPI plus 2%.

## Fees
An annual licence fee, payable in advance, and support fees per Schedule B.

## Liability
The vendor's aggregate liability for all claims arising under this agreement
shall not exceed $250,000. The cap does not apply to breaches of the data
protection clause, for which liability is unlimited.

## Termination
The customer may terminate for convenience at any anniversary on 60 days'
notice.
