# Facilities Management Contract — Osprey Building Services

Vendor reference: OS-9015-B

## Scope
Cleaning, minor repairs and grounds maintenance at the Riverside campus.

## Term
Twelve months, rolling thereafter.

## Fees
Fixed monthly fee plus reactive works at the Schedule C rates.

## Liability
Liability for property damage is capped at $500,000 per incident; liability
for all other claims is capped at the fees paid in the preceding twelve
months.

## Termination
Thirty days' notice by either party.
