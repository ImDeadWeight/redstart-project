# Services Agreement — Alder Logistics Ltd

Vendor reference: AL-2208-M

## Scope
Alder Logistics provides warehousing and last-mile delivery for the Midlands
region, as set out in Schedule 1.

## Term
Twenty-four months from the effective date, renewable by mutual agreement.

## Fees
Monthly fees per Schedule 2, invoiced in arrears, payable within 30 days.

## Liability
Each party's total liability under this agreement is capped at $1,200,000,
except for liability that cannot be limited by law.

## Termination
Either party may terminate on 90 days' written notice.
