# Northwind Field Services — Staff Leave Policy

Version 4.2, effective 1 March 2026. Owner: People Operations.

## 1. Purpose

This policy explains how staff request and take leave, and how managers
approve it. It applies to every permanent and fixed-term employee. Contractors
follow the terms of their own agreements.

## 2. Annual leave entitlement

Full-time staff accrue 25 days of annual leave per year, pro rata for
part-time staff. Leave accrues monthly from the first day of employment. Up to
five unused days may be carried into the next leave year; anything above that
lapses on 31 March unless a director approves an exception in writing.

## 3. Public holidays

Public holidays are in addition to annual leave. Staff who are rostered to
work on a public holiday receive a day off in lieu, to be taken within three
months.

## 4. Requesting leave

All leave is requested through the HR portal. The notice required depends on
the length of the absence:

- Up to three consecutive working days: at least 5 days' notice.
- Four days up to two weeks: at least 10 days' notice.
- More than two weeks: at least 21 days' notice, and the request must name a
  colleague who has agreed to cover urgent work.

Managers respond within three working days. A request that receives no
response in that time is escalated automatically to the manager's own manager.

## 5. Busy periods

Field teams may restrict leave during the two weeks before a scheduled
inspection season. Restrictions are published at least two months ahead.

## 6. Sick leave

Sick leave is separate from annual leave and is covered by the Sickness
Absence Policy. Staff who fall ill during annual leave may convert those days
to sick leave on providing a medical note.

## 7. Unpaid leave

Unpaid leave of up to four weeks may be granted at the manager's discretion
once annual leave for the year is used. Longer unpaid leave needs a director's
approval.

## 8. Review

People Operations reviews this policy every two years, or sooner if the law
changes.
